# Content Style Guide

The reader is assumed to be smart but unfamiliar with AI supply chain details.

## Writing Rules

- Start with the plain-language conclusion.
- Explain technical terms on first use.
- Do not present a big number without explaining what it means.
- Every important number needs an investment interpretation.
- Separate fact, inference, and assumption when judgment is involved.
- Mark rough estimates and low-confidence assumptions clearly.
- Avoid dense tables without narrative.
- Do not write direct buy/sell instructions.

## Number Translation Pattern

```text
Number: AI data center capex rises by X.
Business meaning: More spending flows to GPUs, networking, power, and cooling.
Company meaning: This company benefits only if its product is in the spending path.
Investment meaning: The number matters if it is large relative to company revenue or changes margin/expectations.
```

## Company Status Labels

- `researchable`: worth deeper work under the stated assumptions.
- `wait_for_price`: business is attractive, but valuation setup is not attractive enough.
- `observe`: thesis is plausible but not proven.
- `exclude`: transmission, conversion, valuation, or thesis quality is too weak.
